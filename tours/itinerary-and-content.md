# Itinerary and content

The itinerary and content are what convince travellers to book. Tourtify gives you a structured editor for every part of the tour page, so it looks consistent, reads well on phones and is understood by search engines.

::: info Where to find it
Console › Tours › *tour* › **Itinerary**, **Highlights & FAQs**, **Media** and **Policies** tabs
:::

## Write the itinerary

1. Open the tour and go to the **Itinerary** tab.
2. Choose **Add day**.
3. Fill in the day:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Title | Fly to Lukla, trek to Phakding |
| Description | What happens that day |
| Overnight | Teahouse in Phakding |
| Meals included | Breakfast, lunch, dinner |
| Max altitude (m) | 2,610 |
| Distance (km) | 8 |
| Walking (hours) | 3–4 |

4. Repeat for each day, then choose **Save itinerary**.

Use the day menu to **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** or **Delete** a day.

::: tip Altitude profile
When days have a max altitude, Tourtify draws an **Altitude profile** chart on the tour page automatically.
:::

## Highlights, inclusions and trip facts

On the **Highlights & FAQs** tab:

- **Highlights**: 3–6 short reasons to book, shown at the top of the page.
- **Included** and **Not included**: one item per line, for example "Licensed English-speaking guide" or "International flights".
- **Trip facts**: difficulty (Easy, Moderate, Challenging, Strenuous), max altitude and group size. They appear as quick facts under the title and power search filters.
- **Price shown**: per person or per group, see [Pricing](/tours/pricing).
- **Labels** (up to 3): Best seller, New, Limited spots, Family friendly, Small group.

Choose **Save content**.

## FAQs

Answer the questions travellers always ask: fitness, altitude, gear, refunds. Choose **Add question**, write the question and answer, then **Save FAQs**. FAQs also appear in Google results through FAQ structured data.

## Photos, videos and downloads

On the **Media** tab:

1. **Cover photo & map**: choose a cover photo (shown at the top of the page and in listings). For a map, paste a Google Maps embed link: Google Maps › Share › Embed a map › copy the `src` link.
2. **Photo gallery**: add photos from the [Media library](/website/media-library), add captions and reorder them.
3. **Videos**: paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, or upload a video file.
4. **360° & 3D tours**: paste a Kuula or Matterport link.
5. **Downloads**: offer packing lists, maps or brochures (PDFs or images).
6. Choose **Save media** and **Save downloads**.

::: tip Itinerary PDF
Every tour gets a branded **Itinerary PDF** automatically (cover, facts, day by day, inclusions, FAQs), offered on the tour page. Choose **Preview** in Downloads to check it.
:::

## Good to know

On the **Policies** tab, use **Good to know** for visa, fitness, packing, cancellation and refund notes. It is shown on the tour page.

## Order the page sections

On the **SEO** tab, **Page sections** lets you choose which sections appear on the tour page and in what order, for example moving FAQs above the itinerary or hiding the map.
