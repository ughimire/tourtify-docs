# Places

Places put your itineraries on a map. Add each city, hotel, teahouse, viewpoint, airport or camp once, then pick it for
any day in any tour. Your website shows the route with numbered stops, and the PDF itinerary gets the same map.

::: info Where to find it
Console › Sales › Tours › **Places**
:::

## Add a place

1. Choose **Add place**.
2. Type the name and pick the kind (city or town, hotel, lodge or teahouse, attraction, viewpoint or peak, airport,
   trailhead, camp, restaurant, border crossing).
3. Put it on the map:
   - **Search** finds the place and fills in its position, country and address (when place search is available).
   - Or **click the map** to drop the pin, and drag it to the exact spot. You can also type the latitude and longitude.
4. Add the altitude for mountain trips (the itinerary's checks and the elevation chart use it), a description and
   photos travellers see when they open the place on the map.
5. **Save place**.

You can also add places while you build an itinerary: in the trip builder, the place field searches your places first,
then the place search, and adds a new place in one step. A place added without a position shows **No pin yet** here;
open it and drop the pin.

## Translations

If your website is in more than one language, the place has a name and description per language under
**Translations**. A blank field shows the main-language text.

## Supplier

With Quotes & proposals switched on, link the hotel, lodge or operator you book at this place. Costs for quotes then
come from that supplier.

## Find, filter and see them on a map

- Search by name or address, and filter by kind and country.
- **Map** shows every place with a pin. Click a pin to open it.
- **Used in** shows how many tours use the place; open a place to see which ones.

## Merge duplicates

If the same lodge was added twice, tick both and choose **Merge duplicates**, then pick the one to keep. Every tour that
used the other one now uses it, and it gains the other's translations and photos. The duplicates are deleted.

## Delete a place

Deleting a place removes its pin from the tours that use it. Their days keep the overnight text, so nothing disappears
from your website; the map just has one stop fewer.

## On your website

Tour pages show the itinerary's map: a fast image first, then an interactive map when a traveller taps **Explore the
map** (zoom, pan, and tap a stop for its name, days and altitude). Below the map, the stops are listed in order with the
days spent at each. Search engines and AI assistants get the same route with coordinates.

The map uses map data from OpenStreetMap, served by Tourtify: travellers' browsers don't contact a map company.
