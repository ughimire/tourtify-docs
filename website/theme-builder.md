# Themes and Theme Builder

Your theme decides how your whole website looks: colours, fonts, shapes, the header, the footer and the home page. Pick a ready-made theme in one click, then make it yours in the Theme Builder with a live preview. Nothing changes on your website until you press **Publish**.

::: info Where to find it
Console › Website › Appearance (themes gallery)
Console › Website › Appearance › Customize (Theme Builder)
:::

## Choose a theme

The **Themes** screen shows every theme as a live miniature built from your own content.

| Theme | Tier | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Summit | Free | Clean and bright, big photo, clear prices |
| Expedition | Free | Dark, bold, cinematic: adventure and mountain operators |
| Heritage | Free | Warm serif type: cultural tours, food walks, boutique travel |
| Coastal | Free | Light and airy: beaches, boats, islands, family holidays |
| Jungle | Free | Earthy greens: wildlife, eco and trekking trips |
| Minimal | Free | Black, white and space: lets your photography lead |
| Aurora | Premium <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" /> | Luxury travel, navy and gold |
| Safari | Premium <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" /> | Wildlife, safari and overland |
| Nordic | Premium <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" /> | Ski, fjords, Arctic, city breaks |
| Tropic | Premium <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" /> | Islands, diving and family fun |

1. Go to **Website › Appearance**.
2. Choose **Live preview** on a theme to see it with your content on any page and device.
3. Choose **Activate** and confirm.

Switching keeps your content: hero text and photo, home page blocks, menus and footer. Premium themes also add their signature blocks (for example Stats, Video or Gallery) to your home page; you can hide or remove them afterwards.

::: tip Premium themes on Starter
You can preview premium themes on any plan. Activating one needs the Growth or Scale plan; the button reads **Upgrade to use** until then.
:::

::: warning Unpublished changes
Activating a theme discards any unpublished changes in the Theme Builder. Publish or save them first.
:::

## Open the Theme Builder

Choose **Customize** on the active theme, or go to **Website › Appearance › Customize**. It opens the [visual editor](/website/page-builder) on **Site styles**: the same editor you use for the home page and every builder page, with the real website in the canvas.

- Switch the canvas between **desktop, tablet and mobile**; use the title at the top to switch to the home page, another page, **Edit header** or **Edit footer**.
- Click the header or footer on the canvas to open its builder; colours, fonts and shapes update the canvas instantly.
- **Undo** (⌘Z / Ctrl+Z), **Redo** (⇧⌘Z) and **History** cover site styles and page content together.

| Site styles | What you change |
|---|---|
| Theme presets | Apply another theme's colours, fonts, shapes and layouts. Your content stays. |
| Global colours | Primary and accent colour (or **Use my brand colour**), page background, cards, text, header and footer colours. Dark themes are supported. Elements set to a global colour follow it. |
| Global fonts | Heading and body fonts from the font list or fonts you uploaded. How they load (hosted on Tourtify or from Google Fonts): see [Fonts](./fonts). |
| Buttons & shapes | Corners (Square, Rounded, Pill) and button style (Solid, Outline). |
| Tour cards | Classic, Photo overlay or Minimal cards; show star rating, duration and difficulty, short summary. |
| Header / Footer | The header and footer builders (see below), docked under the canvas. |
| Announcement bar | The message above the header (goes live when saved). |
| Custom CSS | Advanced fine-tuning applied on top of the theme. |

The home page is built with containers and widgets in the same editor (**Widgets** tab). **Publish** puts site styles and the home page live together; **History › Published versions** keeps every published version, with restore.

## Build your header

The header has **three rows**, each with **left, centre and right** zones. Drag elements between zones and click one to edit it.

Elements: Logo, Menu (any menu, nested items show as dropdowns), Search, Phone (tap to call), Email, Button (for example "Book now"), My bookings (traveller sign-in), Currency (with the [Currency switcher](/plugins/currency-switcher) plugin), Social icons and Text.

Row and header options:

- Row height (Slim, Regular, Tall) and own colours per row.
- **Stay on screen when scrolling** (sticky header).
- **Transparent over the hero photo** on the home page.
- Width: Contained or Full width.
- A separate phone header ("Phones") with left and right zones and a menu drawer.

## Build your footer

The footer has up to **three rows of one to four columns**. Drag widgets between columns; click a row name to change its columns, widths, alignment and colours. You can also **Start from a layout**.

Widgets: About, Logo, Menu, Contact, Social, Tours, Destinations, Activities, Categories, Review rating, Secure payments, Text, Button and Copyright (`© {year} {site}` is filled in for you).

The About text and social links come from **Website › Appearance › Site details**; contact details come from **Settings › Business › Business details**. "Secure booking powered by Tourtify" and the cookie settings link always show at the very bottom.

## Arrange home page blocks

Open **Home page**, then **Add a block**. Drag to reorder; click a block to edit it.

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Hero | Big headline, photo and tour search. Layouts: Full photo, Text + photo, Photo + card, Text only. |
| Tours | Your tours as a grid or sliding carousel, with optional filters |
| Destinations / Activities / Tour categories | Photo tiles or a compact list linking to landing pages |
| Why us | Three or four reasons to book with you |
| Reviews | Latest published reviews and your rating |
| Blog | Latest posts |
| Text & image | Your story beside a photo |
| Call to action | A bold band with one button |
| FAQ | Questions as an accordion |
| Gallery | A grid of photos |
| Contact | An enquiry form; messages arrive in Enquiries |
| Stats | Big numbers, e.g. years guiding |
| Video | A YouTube (privacy-enhanced) or Vimeo video |
| Awards & partners | A row of logos |
| Featured testimonial | One big quote from a traveller |

Every block also has **Block style**: background (page, subtle grey, card, brand, contrast or a custom colour, with text contrast handled for you), extra space, **Hide on phones**, **Hide on computers** and an **Anchor** so menus can link to `/#your-anchor`. Use the block menu to **Duplicate**, **Move up/down** or **Remove block**.

## Design the tour page

Every tour page uses one layout, which you design once in the same editor. Open it from Console › Website › Appearance › **Tour page**, or switch to it from the page menu at the top left of the editor. The canvas shows one of your own tours.

The tour page is made of four parts. Each appears once:

| Part | What it shows | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Tour title | Name, rating, summary, labels and key facts | Look (automatic, plain band or over the cover photo), breadcrumb, key facts |
| Tour photos | The tour's gallery (hidden when it has none) | Style, spacing, visibility per device |
| Tour details & booking | Itinerary, inclusions, dates, FAQs and the rest, beside the booking panel | Booking panel on the right or left, section links bar |
| Similar tours | Tours in the same destinations and activities | Heading and how many |

You can do three things with this layout:

- **Move the parts.** Put them in any order, or delete the ones you don't want, such as the photos or similar tours. **Tour details & booking** always stays, because without it nobody could book.
- **Add any widget between them.** For example: trust badges under the title, a "Talk to a trip expert" call to action after the details, or a newsletter sign-up at the bottom.
- **Keep each tour's own section order.** The order of a tour's sections (itinerary, inclusions, FAQs…) is still set on each tour, under its **SEO** tab › Page sections.

The tour page is part of your theme. It saves as a draft with your other changes and goes live when you press **Publish**.

## Save, preview and publish

- Changes **autosave as a draft** ("Draft saved · not live yet"). Visitors see nothing until you publish.
- Choose **Preview** to open the draft in a new tab.
- Choose **Publish** to make the draft live.
- **More actions › Discard draft** throws away all unpublished changes.

If the draft changes in another tab or by a teammate, the builder warns you and lets you **Load their version** or **Keep mine**.

## Restore an earlier version

Each publish is kept in **History** (the last 30). Preview a version, then choose **Restore to draft**, check it and publish.

## Save and share your own themes

- **More actions › Save as a theme…** keeps the current design in **My themes** without publishing.
- On the Themes screen, use **Save a copy**, **Export** (JSON) and **Import theme** to move a design between businesses or keep a backup.
