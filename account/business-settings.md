# Business settings

Business settings hold who you are: your trading name, contact details and branding. They are used on your website, on receipts and PDFs, and in every email you send. Set them first; everything else picks them up.

::: info Where to find it
Console › Settings › Business › Business details
:::

## Business details

1. Go to **Settings**. You land on **Business details**.
2. Fill in each section and save.

| Section | Field | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Your business | Business name | Your trading name on the website, receipts and every email |
| Contact details | Contact email | Shown in email footers. Travellers' replies go here. |
| | Phone or WhatsApp | Email footers, website header and footer |
| | Address | Optional. Helps travellers find your office or meeting point. |
| Brand | Logo | Website header and footer, emails, invoices and PDFs. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background, at least 200 px wide. |
| | Logo for dark backgrounds | Optional white or light version, used automatically on dark headers and footers and over the home page photo |
| | Favicon and app icon | Browser tabs and phones' home screens. Square, at least 512 × 512 px (PNG, SVG or ICO). |
| | Social sharing image | Shown when a page without its own picture is shared (WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, X). 1200 × 630 px. |
| Colour and sign-off | Brand colour | Buttons and accents on emails, receipts and your website |
| | Email sign-off | The last line of every email, e.g. "The Himalaya Treks team" |

### Brand: logo, icon and sharing image

Choose **Upload or choose…** under each item to open your media library: pick a file or upload a new one there, then choose **Use this image**. Each brand item is saved as soon as you choose it, and your website, emails and documents use it straight away (it is a business setting, so it is never part of a website draft). You can set the same items in the [visual editor](/website/page-builder) under **Site styles › Brand**, and see the result on the page as you go.

- **Logo**: SVG logos stay sharp on the website; emails and PDFs get a PNG copy made for you, because many mail apps can't show SVG. Add a **Description** for screen readers.
- **Favicon and app icon**: from one square picture Tourtify makes the browser tab icons (16, 32 and 48 px and a favicon.ico), the 180 px iPhone icon and the 192 and 512 px home-screen icons, including a "maskable" one for Android. If your picture isn't square, you choose the square part with **Crop**.
- **Social sharing image**: pictures of another shape are cropped to 1200 × 630 px; choose the part to show with **Crop**, or later with **Adjust crop**.

Files are checked when you choose them: the type (PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, and ICO for the icon), up to 5 MB, and the size in pixels. SVG files are cleaned of scripts and outside links when they are uploaded. Deleting the original from the media library later doesn't affect your brand: it keeps its own copy.

The **Preview** on the right shows a booking confirmation with your details and branding, and updates as you type. Choose **See and customise every email** to go to [Emails](/bookings/emails).

::: tip Website colours
Your brand colour is the starting point for your theme. In the [visual editor](/website/theme-builder) you can follow it (**Use my brand colour**) or choose separate colours.
:::

## Plan & usage

**Settings › Plan & billing › Plan & usage** shows your current plan, trial days left and how much you use: team members, media storage, connected channels, AI drafts, website assistant answers and e-invoices this month. Only an owner can change the plan; the change takes effect immediately. See [Plans & billing](/get-started/plans-and-billing).

::: warning Paused accounts
If a trial ends without a plan, online booking pauses. Your website and data are safe; choose a plan to start taking bookings again.
:::

## Who can change settings

Owners and admins can view and change business settings. Only the owner can connect payment accounts and manage billing.
