## Free trial

- New businesses start with a 30-day free trial. You can try every feature during the trial.
- **Plan & usage** shows how many days are left. You can subscribe any time; you aren't charged before the trial ends.
- If the trial ends without a plan, online booking **pauses**. Your website and data are kept safe, and nothing is deleted.

## Change your plan

Open **Settings › Plan & billing › Plan & usage** to see your plan and what you use: team members, media storage, connected channels, AI drafts, website assistant answers and e-invoices this month. Only an owner can change the plan.

- **Upgrades and downgrades take effect immediately.** The difference for the rest of the billing period is added to, or credited on, your next invoice.
- Before a downgrade, the console lists the features that will be paused. Plugins your new plan doesn't include show **Paused · needs …**; their settings and data are kept and they resume when you upgrade again.
- If you have more team members than the new plan allows, remove some before downgrading.
- **Switch to yearly billing** or **Switch to monthly billing** changes how often you pay.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Use **Manage billing** to update your card, see invoices or cancel. Where online card billing isn't available, choosing a plan sends you an invoice with a payment reference (bank transfer), and your plan starts when the payment reaches us.

## Pausing and cancelling

You can cancel at any time in **Manage billing**; the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then online booking pauses. A paused business keeps its tours, bookings, customers, pages and media. Choose a plan again and online booking resumes straight away.

If a payment fails, the console shows the date to update your card by. Tourtify retries the payment automatically.

## Payment processing fees

Tourtify doesn't charge commission or transaction fees on your bookings. Your payment provider (Stripe, PayPal or any other provider you connect) charges its own processing fee, which you agree with them directly.
