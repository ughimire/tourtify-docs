# Your console

The console at `app.tourtify.com` is where you and your team run the business. It works on a laptop and on a phone, in light or dark mode, and in fifteen languages.

## The dashboard

The dashboard answers "what needs my attention today?":

- **Setup checklist** until your business is ready: create your first tour, set prices, add departure dates, connect payments, publish your tour, use your own domain, invite your team, share your booking site.
- **Today and upcoming departures** with seats booked, held and remaining.
- **Revenue this month**, recent bookings and **balances** due soon or overdue.
- Items that need attention, such as payments to review.

## The sidebar

The sidebar follows the order of an operator's day: sell, run the trips, get paid, then the website and growing the business. Plugins add their pages to the section they belong to, never a new section of their own, and only while they are switched on.

| Section | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** and **Inbox** | Today, upcoming departures, recent bookings, the setup checklist; the shared [Inbox](/plugins/inbox) when it is on |
| **Sales** | **Bookings** (All bookings, Waitlist, Travel insurance, Abandoned checkouts), **Enquiries** (Enquiries, Quotes), **Customers** (All customers, Company accounts), **Tours** (All tours, Destinations, Activities, Categories, Library, Add-ons & extras, Hotel rooms, External booking links), **Pricing** (Discounts, Booking fees), **Point of sale** and **Rentals & passes** |
| **Operations** | **Departures**, **Check-in today**, **Resources & tasks** (Operations calendar, Schedule, Conflicts, Tasks), **Tour guides**, **Trekking**, **Scan tickets**, **Waivers** and **Suppliers** (Suppliers, Supplier bookings, Allotments) |
| **Finance** | **Payments** (All payments, Disputes, Refund approvals, Payment links, Balances, Invoices, Gift vouchers, Payouts, Supplier bills, Bank reconciliation) and **Reports** (Reports, Accounting, FX gain & loss, TCS for India, Costs & profit) |
| **Website** | **Pages** (Permalinks, Redirects), **Blog** (Categories, Tags), **Media**, **Appearance** (Customize, Menus, Announcement bar, Accessibility, Site details) and **Traffic & logs** (Protection) |
| **Growth** | **Marketing** (Reviews, Email campaigns, Mailing list, Segments, Automations) and **Sales channels** (Channels, Agents, Google Things to do) |
| **Settings** | Business, Team & access, Payments & tax, Bookings & travellers, Website, Notifications & messages, Integrations & developers, Plan & billing, Privacy & data, and Your account |
| **Plugins** | Every plugin, with switches and settings |

What you see depends on your [role](/account/team-and-roles): a guide might only see bookings and the ticket scanner, while an owner sees everything.

## Search and the command palette

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows, or <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere to open the command palette. Search bookings by reference or traveller name, jump to a tour, or go straight to any page.

Every main list (bookings, customers, payments and more) can save its filters and columns as a view. See [Saved list views](./saved-list-views).

## Several businesses

If you run more than one brand, each is a separate business with its own website, team and data. Switch between them with the business switcher at the top of the sidebar.

## Working on a phone

The console is fully usable at phone width. Guides can open the day's manifest and [scan QR tickets](/plugins/qr-tickets) at the meeting point with their phone camera.
