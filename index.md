## Before you begin

Tourtify is hosted software: there is nothing to install. You need a modern browser (the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and an email address. Your website, SSL certificate, backups and updates are handled for you.

::: tip New here?
Every new business starts with a **30-day free trial with no card required**. Follow the [Quick start](/get-started/quick-start) and you can take a real booking in under an hour.
:::

<PlanTable />

All plans include unlimited tours, unlimited bookings and 0% booking commission. [Compare plans in detail](/get-started/plans-and-billing).
