# Complaints

The complaint log keeps every complaint in one place, however it reached you: by email, phone, in person, on your website or in a review. Each complaint gets a reference, an owner and two deadlines: one to acknowledge it and one to give a full answer. When it is answered, you record what you did and any compensation.

The log is on every plan. Package travel laws such as the EU Package Travel Directive ask organisers to deal with complaints promptly, and travellers everywhere expect an answer, so it is useful whether or not you sell packages.

::: info Where to find it
Console › Sales › Customers › Complaints
:::

## Log a complaint

1. Go to **Complaints** and choose **Log complaint**. From a booking, choose **More actions › Log a complaint…**: the form starts with that booking's traveller.
2. Enter who complained and, if you have it, their email. A complaint with an email shows on that customer's timeline.
3. Choose **How it came in**, **What it's about** and the **Severity**.
4. Write a one-line **Summary** and, if you like, the **Details** in their words.
5. Change **Received on** if the complaint arrived on an earlier day. The deadlines count from that day.
6. Choose **Log complaint**. It gets the next reference (CMP-0001, CMP-0002…) and is assigned to you unless you pick another owner.

## Deadlines

| Deadline | Default | Set in |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledge by | 7 days after it was received | Settings › Package travel › Complaints |
| Answer by | 60 days after it was received | Settings › Package travel › Complaints |

The list shows the deadline that matters now: **Acknowledge by** while the complaint is open, then **Answer by**. A deadline due today shows in amber; a missed one shows **Acknowledgement overdue** or **Answer overdue** in red. Use the **Overdue** button to see only those.

Changing the days in settings applies to complaints logged from then on. Complaints already logged keep their deadlines.

## Work through a complaint

Click a complaint to open it.

| Status | Meaning | How it gets there |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Logged, not yet acknowledged | **Log complaint** |
| Acknowledged | You told the traveller you are looking into it | **Mark acknowledged** |
| Resolved | Answered, with the outcome recorded | Write the **Resolution** and choose **Mark resolved** |
| Closed | Nothing more to do | **Close** |

- **Resolution** is required: write what you told the traveller and what you did. Add **Compensation** if you paid or refunded something; it is recorded in your main currency. To refund money, use the booking's **Refund…** action as usual.
- **Close** an open complaint only when no answer is needed (for example, it was withdrawn). Tourtify asks first.
- **Reopen** a resolved or closed complaint if the traveller comes back. It returns to Acknowledged (or Open if it never was), and the deadlines stay as they were.
- **Edit** changes the details, the owner or the day it was received. A new received day moves the deadlines.

## Find complaints

Use the tabs (**All**, **Open**, **Resolved**, **Closed**), **Search complaints** (reference, name, email or summary) and **Overdue**. Open includes acknowledged complaints.

A booking's complaints show in a **Complaints** panel on the booking page. Each one opens in the log.

## Who can see complaints

Complaints hold customer details, so they follow the same access as the customer list: owners, admins and sales agents can see and work on them; guides can't. Logging, acknowledging, resolving, closing and reopening also need permission to update bookings. Every step is recorded in the activity log.

When you erase a traveller's data (Settings › Privacy), their complaints are deleted too, and a data download includes them.
