# Travel insurance <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" />

Travel insurance offers cancellation and travel cover at checkout from a licensed insurer, priced for each trip and traveller. The insurer sells the policy and handles claims. You earn commission, and travellers book with confidence. If you already work with an insurer or broker, you can link to them at checkout instead.

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** Growth and Scale
- **Category:** Sales
- **Where:** Plugins › Travel insurance, and Sales › Bookings › Travel insurance
:::

## How it works

**What the traveller sees**

- At checkout, a cover section under your headline (by default "Protect your trip").
- They say where the travellers live, and see the cover options priced for their trip, with links to the **Policy terms** and **Key information**.
- Cover is opt-in: nothing is pre-selected, and **No cover, thanks** is always an option.
- The cover price is added to their booking total.

**After booking**

- The insurer issues the policy once the booking is paid.
- If the booking is cancelled or the unpaid checkout expires, the policy is cancelled too.
- Claims go to the insurer, not to you.

**What your team sees**

**Sales › Bookings › Travel insurance** lists every policy with its booking, the number of policies and the cover sold. Each policy shows its status: **Waiting for payment**, **Policy issued**, **Not issued**, **Cancelled** or **Lapsed**.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Plugins** in the console.
2. Find **Travel insurance** and flip its switch on.
3. Select **Configure** and choose your insurer, then **Save**.

The plugin card shows which insurer is in use and how many policies have been issued. If Tourtify's insurance partnership isn't live in your region yet, it tells you so: add your own XCover partner keys in the drawer, or choose **Your own insurer**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Offer headline | Shown above the cover options at checkout. | Protect your trip |
| Insurer | **XCover by Cover Genius**, **Your own insurer (a link at checkout)**, or **Test insurer (not in production)**. XCover sells the cover under its own licences, through Tourtify's partnership or your own XCover partner keys. The test insurer is for trying it out and never runs in production. | XCover by Cover Genius |

**Your own insurer**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Your insurer's page | Where travellers buy cover from your own insurer or broker (your affiliate link works). Shown at checkout when the insurer is "Your own insurer". | Empty |
| What to tell travellers | One or two sentences above the link. | We recommend travel insurance that covers cancellation and medical costs. |

## Good to know

- With **Your own insurer**, travellers buy cover on your insurer's site. Nothing is added to the booking and no policy shows in Tourtify.
- Cover is priced outside your tour's tax.
- Turning the plugin off: cover stops being offered at checkout. Policies already sold stay with the insurer.
