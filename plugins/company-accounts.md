# Company accounts <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" />

Company accounts let schools, companies and partner agencies book on account and pay you later on terms such as net 30, up to a credit limit you set. Invoices go to the company, and you get account statements, ageing and automatic overdue reminders.

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** Growth and Scale
- **Category:** Payments
- **Where:** Plugins › Company accounts, and Customers › Company accounts
:::

## How it works

1. You add a company with its billing details, payment terms and credit limit.
2. On a confirmed booking, you choose **Put on account** and pick the company. The booking is billed to the company and falls due on its terms.
3. The account shows what is owed and how late it is. While it has overdue bookings, its billing email gets a statement on a schedule you set.
4. When the company pays, record the payment on the booking as usual.

The company doesn't see anything new on your website: putting a booking on account is done by your team in the console.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Plugins** in the console.
2. Find **Company accounts** and flip its switch on.
3. Select **Configure** to set default terms and reminders, then **Save**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Default payment terms for new accounts | **Due on receipt**, **Net 7**, **Net 14**, **Net 15**, **Net 30**, **Net 45**, **Net 60** or **Net 90**. You can change the terms on each account. | Net 30 |
| Send overdue statements every (days) | While an account has overdue bookings, its billing email gets a statement this often. | 7 |
| Note on statements | How to pay you, e.g. bank details and the reference to quote. | Empty |

## Using Company accounts

Go to **Customers › Company accounts**.

**Add an account**

Choose **New account** and fill in the company name, **Billing email**, **Contact**, **Address**, **Tax ID**, **Terms**, **Credit limit** (or **No limit**), **Overdue reminders** (**On** or **Off**) and **Notes**. An account is billed in one currency.

**The accounts list**

Search by **Name or billing email**. Each account shows its terms, what is **Owed** and how much is **Overdue**, and **Credit used** against the limit ("{used} of {limit} credit").

**An account's page**

- **Open bookings**: bookings on the account that aren't paid yet, with their due dates.
- **Ageing**: what is owed, by how late it is: **Not yet due**, **1–30 days late**, **31–60 days late**, **61–90 days late** and **Over 90 days**.
- **Activity, last 90 days**: bookings, payments, refunds and cancellations.
- **Statement**: choose a period (**From**, **To**) for activity, open bookings and ageing. Download **Statement PDF** or **Statement CSV**, or **Email statement** to the billing email.
- **Edit account** to change details, or close the account.

## Good to know

- Only confirmed bookings can go on a company account.
- A booking can't go on account if it would take the company over its credit limit, if the booking is in a different currency from the account, or if the account is closed (reopen it first).
- Turning the plugin off: companies can no longer book on account. Accounts, statements and open balances are kept.
