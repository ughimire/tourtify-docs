# AI assistant <Badge type="info" text="All plans" />

The AI assistant writes first drafts for your team: replies to enquiries, inbox messages and reviews, and tour summaries, highlights, SEO text and translations, from your own tour details, in your voice and the traveller's language. Drafts always wait for you to check them; nothing is sent or published by itself.

Optionally, the website assistant answers your website chat instantly from your published tours, dates and prices, and hands over to your team in the [Inbox](./inbox).

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** Starter, Growth and Scale (your own Anthropic key: Scale)
- **Category:** Website
- **Where:** Plugins › AI assistant, plus AI buttons across the console
:::

## How it works

**Where the AI buttons appear**

| Where | Button | What it drafts |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiries, in **Quick reply** | **Draft with AI** | A reply to the enquiry, with a subject. |
| Inbox, in a conversation | **Draft with AI** | A reply to the customer. Needs the [Inbox](./inbox). |
| Reviews, when replying | **Draft with AI** | A public response to the review. |
| Tour editor, **Summary** | **Write summary with AI** | The tour summary. |
| Tour editor, **Highlights** | **Suggest with AI** | 3–6 short reasons to book. |
| Tour editor, SEO | **Write with AI** | How the tour appears on Google. |
| Translation pages for tours, library, pages and site text | **Translate with AI** | Fills only empty fields unless you tick **Replace existing**. Each run counts as one AI draft. |

Select a draft button and, if you like, add a note under **Anything to include?** (for example "offer the 14 March departure"), then **Write draft**. Drafts use your tour details and real dates. You edit the draft before anything is sent or saved.

**The website assistant**

With **Answer website chat instantly with AI** on, visitors who open "Message us" on your website get an answer straight away:

- Answers come only from your published tours, prices, dates and pages. Prices and dates are checked against your data before the answer is shown; an answer that doesn't match is replaced with a safe one.
- The assistant never sees your customers' names, emails or bookings.
- Changes to bookings, cancellations, refunds, payments and requests for a person always go to your team, as do the topics you list. The visitor is asked for their name and email, and the whole chat lands in the Inbox.
- When it hands over, the visitor is told when your team is in, based on your chat hours.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Plugins** in the console.
2. Find **AI assistant** and flip its switch on.
3. Select **Configure**, choose your tone of voice and describe your business, then **Save**.
4. For the website assistant: turn on the [Inbox](./inbox) plugin with "Message us" on, then switch on **Answer website chat instantly with AI**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Tone of voice | **Warm and friendly**, **Professional and concise**, **Energetic and adventurous** or **Refined and premium**. | Warm and friendly |
| About your business (used in every draft) | What makes your trips special, how you sign off, words to use or avoid. | Empty |

**Website assistant**

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Answer website chat instantly with AI | Needs the Inbox plugin with "Message us" on. Answers come only from your published tours, prices, dates and pages; anything else goes to your team in the Inbox, with the whole chat. | Off |
| Greeting | The first message visitors see. | Hi! Ask me anything about our tours, dates and prices. |
| Topics the assistant never discusses | For example: visas, medical advice, competitors. Visitors asking about these are passed to your team. | Empty |
| Always hand these to your team | For example: private trips, group quotes, complaints. Changes to bookings, cancellations, refunds and payments always go to your team. | Empty |
| Your team answers chats on | The days your team is in. | Monday to Friday |
| From / Until | 24-hour time, in your business's time zone. | 09:00 – 18:00 |

**Your own AI account** <Badge type="warning" text="Scale" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Anthropic API key | From console.anthropic.com › API keys. Checked with Anthropic when you save, stored encrypted and never shown again. Remove it to go back to Tourtify's AI and your plan's monthly allowance. |

## Monthly limits

| | Starter | Growth | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI drafts a month | 20 | 300 | 2,000 |
| Website assistant answers a month | 200 | 2,000 | 10,000 |

After each draft, the console shows how many AI drafts are left this month.

On Scale, **Your own Anthropic key** sends this business's AI requests to your own Anthropic account: billed by Anthropic under your own agreement, with no Tourtify monthly limits.

## Good to know

- The website assistant needs the Inbox, which is on Growth and Scale. On Starter you can use the drafting buttons.
- Always read a draft before you send or publish it.
- Turning the plugin off: AI drafting and the website assistant stop. Your settings are kept.
