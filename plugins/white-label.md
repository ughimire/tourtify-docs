# White label <Badge type="warning" text="Scale" />

White label removes every "powered by Tourtify" mention from your website, emails, PDFs and proposals, so travellers only ever see your brand.

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** Scale
- **Category:** Website
- **Where:** Plugins › White label
:::

## How it works

Without the plugin, a small "Secure booking powered by Tourtify" line appears in your website footer, and emails to travellers end with "Booking system by Tourtify". With White label on:

- Your website footer shows only your own text and links.
- Emails to travellers carry only your branding.
- Booking PDFs, invoices and proposals sent with [Quotes & proposals](./quotes) carry only your brand.

There is nothing else to set up. Your logo, colours and email branding stay as you set them in the console.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Plugins** in the console.
2. Find **White label** and flip its switch on.

The plugin has no settings.

## Good to know

- Use a [custom domain](/website/custom-domains) and [your own email server](./own-email-server) as well, so links and sender addresses carry your brand too.
- Turning the plugin off: "Powered by Tourtify" shows again on your website and in emails.
