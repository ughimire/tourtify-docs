# Quotes & proposals <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" />

Quotes & proposals is for tailor-made and private trips. Build a day-by-day itinerary from your suppliers' rates, see your margin as you go, and send a proposal. The traveller accepts it online and it becomes a booking, with the deposit to pay.

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** Growth and Scale
- **Category:** Sales
- **Where:** Plugins › Quotes & proposals, and Sales › Enquiries › Quotes
:::

## How it works

**What the traveller sees**

- An email with your personal note and a link to view the proposal online.
- The proposal: your welcome message, the trip day by day, the price, your terms, and when it's valid until. If you offer options (for example Standard, Superior, Luxury), they pick one.
- They accept online and pay the deposit, or decline and tell you why. The accepted proposal becomes a booking.

**What your team sees**

**Sales › Enquiries › Quotes** lists every quote with its status and the totals: **Open quotes**, **Value in play**, **Margin in play** and **Won (90 days)**. You can see when a traveller opened a proposal.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Plugins** in the console.
2. Find **Quotes & proposals** and flip its switch on.
3. Select **Configure** to set your default margin, deposit, validity and terms, then **Save**.
4. To take deposits online, connect a payment account in **Settings › Payments & tax › Payments**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Default margin on supplier costs (%) | Sell prices follow this margin unless you set them by hand. | 25 |
| Deposit to confirm (%) | What the traveller pays when they accept. | 30 |
| Proposals are valid for (days) | After this, the quote expires. | 14 |
| Terms shown on every proposal | Payment terms, cancellation policy, what happens if prices change. | Empty |

## Build a quote

Select **New quote** on the Quotes page, or **Create quote** from an enquiry or an Inbox conversation.

1. **Trip**: the trip name, the traveller's name, email and phone, adults and children, the start date and time, and **Valid until**. Add a **Welcome message** shown at the top of the proposal.
2. **Itinerary**: one entry per day, with a day title, what happens and where they stay overnight. **Add day** for each day.
3. **Options** (optional): **Offer options** to give two to four versions of the trip, for example Standard, Superior and Luxury. Mark one as **Recommended**. Without options, there is one price for the whole trip.
4. **Services and prices**: what you pay suppliers, and what the traveller pays. Add a service to a day or to the whole trip, with a category (**Accommodation**, **Transport**, **Guide**, **Activity**, **Permits & fees**, **Meals**, **Flights** or **Other**), quantity, unit cost and unit sell price. Sell prices follow your margin unless you set them by hand.
5. **Add from a supplier** pulls in a contract rate from [Suppliers](./suppliers), priced for the start date. Per-person rates are added for all travellers.
6. **Terms** and **Price**: your costs, what the traveller pays, per person, margin and margin %, the currency, **Deposit %** and **Balance due (days before)**.
7. **Save quote**, then **Preview** it.

## Send the proposal

Select **Send proposal**, add a personal note if you like, and **Send**. The proposal is emailed to the traveller with a link to view and accept it online. You can also **Copy link** or **Open proposal**, and **Send again** later.

Each quote moves through **Draft**, **Sent**, **Accepted**, **Declined** or **Expired**. When accepted, **Open the booking** takes you to the new booking. A declined quote shows the traveller's reason.

From a quote you can also **Duplicate** it or **Delete quote**.

## Good to know

- If online payment isn't set up, you can still send a proposal. Travellers are asked to contact you instead of paying online until a payment account is connected.
- With the [Deposits](./deposits) plugin off, travellers pay in full when they accept.
- Turning the plugin off: proposal links you've sent stop opening and can't be accepted. Your quotes are kept.
