# Resellers & agents <Badge type="tip" text="Growth+" />

Resellers & agents is for the travel agents, hotel concierges and affiliates who send you bookings. Each one gets their own referral link and commission rate. Bookings they send are credited to them automatically, and you see what you owe each one, with statements and payouts. Agents can also sign in to a partner portal to book for their clients.

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** Growth and Scale
- **Category:** Distribution
- **Where:** Plugins › Resellers & agents, and Growth › Sales channels › Agents
:::

## How it works

**Referral links**

- Each agent has a referral link to your website with their code in it (`?ref=CODE`).
- When a traveller arrives through the link, your website remembers the code on their device for the number of days you set. A booking made in that time is credited to the agent.
- You can also credit an agent by hand on any booking.

**Commission**

Each agent has a commission rate. Their confirmed bookings add up their sales, the commission they've earned, and what you still owe them.

**The partner portal**

Agents you invite sign in to their own portal with a one-time email link (it works once, for 15 minutes). There they can:

- See their commission rate, what they've earned, what's been paid and what they're still owed.
- Copy their referral link.
- See upcoming and past bookings, with a **Confirmation PDF** for each.
- Make a **New booking** for a client: choose the tour, option and date, the travellers, and the client's details. The form shows the **Client price**, **Your commission** and **Net you owe**. The client gets the booking confirmation by email; payment is on the agent's account and you collect it as usual.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Plugins** in the console.
2. Find **Resellers & agents** and flip its switch on.
3. Select **Configure** to set the default commission and the referral window, then **Save**.
4. Open **Growth › Sales channels › Agents** and add your agents.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Default commission for new agents (%) | The rate filled in when you add an agent. You can change it per agent. | 10 |
| Credit the agent for bookings made within (days of the click) | Your website remembers the referral code on the traveller's device for this long. | 30 |

## Manage agents

Open **Growth › Sales channels › Agents**.

**Add an agent**

Select **Add agent** and fill in **Name**, **Company** (optional), **Commission (%)**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Referral code** (made from their name if empty) and **Notes** (payment details, agreement, contact person). Keep **Active** on so new bookings through their link are credited to them.

**Performance**

The **Performance** view shows **Bookings from agents**, **Sales**, **Commission earned** and **Owed to agents (all time)** for the dates you choose. Each agent shows their bookings, earned and owed amounts. A paused agent is marked **Paused**.

**Agent actions**

- **Copy referral link**
- **View bookings**
- **Statement (PDF)**, **Statement (CSV)** or **Email statement**
- **Send portal invite**: emails the agent a link to the partner portal
- **Record payout**: what you paid the agent outside Tourtify (bank transfer, cash…), with amount, currency and reference
- **Payout history**: payouts you recorded, newest first. **Undo payout** makes the commission owed again.
- **Edit**

## Good to know

- Payouts are recorded, not sent: you pay agents your usual way and record it here.
- The referral cookie is a marketing cookie: it is cleared when a visitor turns marketing cookies off. See [Cookie consent and tracking](/website/cookie-consent-and-tracking).
- Marketplaces such as GetYourGuide and Viator connect through the [Channel manager](./channel-manager) instead.
- Turning the plugin off: agents can't sign in to their portal and referral links stop earning commission. Agents, commissions and statements are kept.
