# Sales pipeline <Badge type="info" text="All plans" />

Sales pipeline turns your enquiries into a sales pipeline, on the Enquiries page you already use. Set a date to get back to each traveller, and whoever handles the enquiry gets a reminder that morning. Record what each trip is worth and why enquiries were lost. On Growth, see the whole pipeline as a board with the money at every stage and your win rate.

::: info At a glance
- **Plan:** All plans (the board and win rate: Growth and Scale)
- **Category:** Sales
- **Where:** Console › Plugins › Sales pipeline, and Sales › Enquiries
- **Works with:** [Quotes & proposals](./quotes), [Inbox](./inbox), [Email campaigns](./campaigns)
:::

## How it works

1. Open an enquiry. Under **Pipeline**, choose **Follow up on** and, if you know it, the **Trip value** in your main currency.
2. At 8:00 in your business's time zone, everyone with follow-ups due that day, or overdue, gets one email listing them. Only enquiries with someone in **Handled by** are included.
3. **Follow-ups due** above the list shows everything due today or overdue, oldest first. Next to each enquiry you see **Follow up today**, **Follow-up overdue** or the day.
4. When you mark an enquiry **Won** or **Lost**, its follow-up date is cleared. For a lost enquiry, choose **Why it was lost**: price or budget, no dates that suit, booked with someone else, stopped replying, not a trip you run, postponed their trip, or other. Reopening an enquiry clears the reason.

### Pipeline board (Growth)

Switch the Enquiries page to **Board** to see your enquiries in columns: New, Contacted, Won and Lost. Each column shows how many enquiries it holds and what they're worth. Won and lost show the last 90 days. Above the board:

- **Open pipeline:** the value of every new and contacted enquiry.
- **Win rate, last 90 days:** won ÷ (won + lost).
- **Why enquiries were lost:** your three most common reasons.

Click a card to open the enquiry.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Console › Plugins** and open **Sales pipeline**.
2. Turn it on. The morning reminder is on by default. Switch **Morning reminder email of follow-ups due** off if your team works from the list instead.

## Good to know

- Follow-up dates are calendar days in your business's time zone.
- Trip value is an estimate for your pipeline. It doesn't change prices, quotes or bookings.
- Turning the plugin off hides the fields and the board. Your dates, values and reasons are kept, and come back when you turn it on again.
- AI apps connected through [MCP](./mcp) can read your enquiries as before.
