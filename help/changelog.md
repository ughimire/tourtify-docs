# Changelog

New features and notable improvements, newest first.

## October 2026

### 21 October
- **Finer [team permissions](/account/team-and-roles#permissions):** exporting customer contact details, publishing the website, deleting tours and pages, sending campaigns, replying to and moderating reviews, and sales reports are now separate permissions. Everyone keeps what they could do before.

### 20 October
- **[Permalinks](/website/permalinks):** rename your website's address bases, such as `/tours` to `/trips`, with 301 redirects from every old address.
- **[My bookings](/bookings/customers#the-traveller-portal-my-bookings):** travellers can set a password, or keep signing in with an emailed link.
- **[Website languages](/website/languages):** translated pages now show everything in their language (tour lists, posts, footer, related tours), blog posts are served at their translated address, and tour pages use their translated SEO title and description.
- The console and super admin work on tablets and phones.

### 19 October
- **Translate the home page, menus, announcement bar and footer** for each [website language](/website/languages).
- **[Suppliers & contracts](/plugins/suppliers)**, finance controls and scheduling conflicts on [Resources & tasks](/plugins/resources-and-scheduling).
- Payment providers connect in one of two ways: a one-click connection where the provider offers it, or your own API keys.

### 18 October
- **Plugins merged so each feature has one place:** promo codes, automatic discounts, group discounts and dynamic pricing are now one [Discounts](/plugins/discounts) plugin; deposits and instalment plans are [Deposits & instalments](/plugins/deposits); the WhatsApp chat button and notifications are one [WhatsApp](/plugins/whatsapp-button) plugin; abandoned checkout recovery is part of [Email automations](/plugins/automations).
- **Package travel** settings (Settings › Bookings & travellers › Package travel) and a reusable tour content library (Sales › Tours › Library).

### 17 October
- **Local [payment providers](/plugins/payment-providers):** Mercado Pago, Paystack, Flutterwave, iyzico, KOMOJU, PayMongo, Opn Payments, Tap Payments, eSewa, Fonepay, Xendit, Mollie and Square, alongside Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay and Khalti.
- The console in fifteen languages and websites in many more. See [Console languages](/account/console-languages).
- **Trip builder:** itinerary builder, [places and maps](/tours/places), formatted descriptions.

### 16 October
- **[E-invoicing](/plugins/invoices#e-invoicing)** with country formats, the tax engine and regional privacy and consent settings.
- **[Channel manager](/plugins/channel-manager)** for OTAs, [traffic and protection](/website/traffic-and-protection) for your website.

### 13–15 October
- **New plugins:** the shared [Inbox](/plugins/inbox), [Email campaigns](/plugins/campaigns), [Rentals, passes & memberships](/plugins/rentals-and-passes), [Trekking & expeditions](/plugins/trekking), [Point of sale](/plugins/point-of-sale), [Google Things to do](/plugins/google-things-to-do) and the [AI assistant](/plugins/ai-assistant) for your website chat.
- **One visual editor** for your theme and pages, with 60 sections. See [Visual editor & widgets](/website/page-builder).
- A field staff app for guides and check-in staff that works offline.

### 12 October
- **Booking on your website, reworked:** clearer availability calendar, cart and checkout, tour media and honest, all-in pricing.
- **New plugin: [QR tickets & check-in](/plugins/qr-tickets).** Every traveller gets a QR ticket; scan it with a phone at the meeting point. Duplicates, cancelled bookings and the wrong day are caught.
- **[Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in)** with an authenticator app and recovery codes.
- **[Console languages](/account/console-languages):** the console in eight languages, including right-to-left.
- **Access groups:** custom team roles with per-permission grants.
- Payment review alerts and safer CSV exports.

### 11 October
- **New plugin: [Dynamic pricing](/plugins/dynamic-pricing)** — early-bird, last-minute, occupancy and date-based prices.
- **Plan & usage** page: your plan, usage against limits, and plan comparison.
- **Plugins** marketplace with a settings drawer for every plugin; deeper features across all plugins.
- Rebuilt operations screens: bookings, departures, customers, enquiries and reviews.
- New dashboard and reports.
- Fresh console design with a light, colourful theme.

### 9 October
- **Import from WordPress** and WP Travel Engine, with website redirects. See [Move from WordPress](/get-started/import-from-wordpress).
- **New plugin: [WhatsApp notifications](/plugins/whatsapp-notifications)** from your own WhatsApp Business number.
- **SEO score** with a focus keyword for tours, pages and posts.
- **Theme Builder v2:** header and footer builders, click-to-edit, undo/redo, publish history and restore.
- **Destinations, activities and tour categories** with their own landing pages.
- Premium themes with live preview.
- **Taxes:** add VAT, GST or sales tax.
- Booking page: record offline payments, resend emails and add internal notes.
