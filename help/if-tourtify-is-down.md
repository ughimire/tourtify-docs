# If Tourtify is unreachable

Tours run whether or not a website is up. Set up the items below once. If Tourtify, or the internet at your meeting
point, then goes down, your team still has the next week's departures, traveller names and phone numbers, and every
booking in their inbox.

## Before it happens: three minutes of setup

1. **Install the Today app on every phone that runs tours.** Open **Today** (or **Check-in today**) in the console on
   the phone and add it to the home screen. Departures in the next 7 days (for guides, the ones they're assigned to)
   are kept on the device, ready to use offline, with each traveller, their lead contact and phone, what's left to
   collect and the check-in list. Open it once a day while online so the copy stays current.
2. **Make sure your team gets booking emails.** Owners and admins get an email for every confirmed booking. Those
   emails stay in your inbox, so every booking's details can still be searched there.
3. **Keep your own copy.** Download the CSV exports before busy periods, or on a routine:
   - **Bookings › Export** for all bookings;
   - **Departures › Export**, and each departure's **manifest** (CSV) for its traveller list;
   - **Customers › Export** for contact details;
   - invoices and balances from their own pages.

   With **Reports › Scheduled emails** you can email yourself a daily or weekly summary as PDF and CSV.

## While it's down

- **Run today's tours from the Today app.** It opens without a connection. Check travellers in as usual: check-ins are
  saved on the phone and sync when the connection is back. Don't remove the offline copy until they have synced.
- **Find a booking** in the team booking emails, or in your latest export.
- **Travellers** have their own confirmation email with the booking reference, date, meeting point and what they paid,
  so they can show it at check-in.
- **New bookings** can't be taken on your website while it's down. Write down any taken by phone and add them in
  **Bookings › New booking** once you're back. Seats are checked again then, so nothing is double-booked.

## Your data is safe

- Your data is backed up every night, encrypted, and kept off-site for 30 days. A restore is tested every week.
- Updates are rolled out without downtime. If one goes wrong, the previous version is put back.
- You can export your bookings, customers and reports at any time, and your data stays yours. To get a full copy of
  everything in your account, [contact support](./support).

If the console or your website seems down, check your own internet connection first, then
[contact support](./support) with the time it started and what you see.
